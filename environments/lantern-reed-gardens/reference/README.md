# Lantern Reed Gardens reference

- `world-setting.png`: visual direction for the basin, plant families, mineral shelves, leaf-roof shelter and native animals.
- `checkpoint-vista.png`: the working environment at the dusk Causeway view, captured on 1 November 2026. Reproduce it with the scenery browser test in `../dev/scenery.residents.spec.ts`.

The concept is a direction for shapes and materials, not a promise of pixel-identical terrain. Keep pale chipped rock distinct from lavender gravel, mint stems, thin veined leaves, fibrous timber, woven blue wall panels and folded lantern husks. Lanterns need dark ribs and a small warm luminous core. Native fauna are Poolglass swimmers, Reed Waders and four-winged Lamp Petals; the three Muse residents support greeting, petting, following and naming.

The checkpoint records the final visual revision and full-resolution High setting. Cliffs and canopies are simplified for procedural geometry. Dense planting must leave open swimming channels and dry resident routes. The environment README records the separate performance limits and the local basin-reflection approximation.
