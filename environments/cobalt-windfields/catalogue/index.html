06About this edition

Reading the catalogue

A visual record of the current local environment, grounded in its active geometry and scene. The catalogue adds no gameplay changes.

In the world

Planet views, landscapes and portraits retain the actual scene’s light, haze, framing and atmosphere. They show the current planting, geology and white clouds with a faint yellow tint.

In the studio

Source geometry is isolated under fixed light, without aerial fog, live articulation, growth deformation or post-processing. Rock and coral plates use their detailed geometry. Every subject is fitted separately.

One form, many instances

Thirteen rock prototypes and two main botanical families underpin the scenery. Each plant family has three habits. Inventory numbers refer to source forms, not placed population counts; distant coral is an alternate render mesh.
