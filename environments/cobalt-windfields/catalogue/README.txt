COBALT WINDFIELDS - VISUAL CATALOGUE
Compiled 2 November 2026

Open index.html in a browser for the browsable catalogue, or read
output/pdf/cobalt-windfields-catalogue.pdf for the 23-page edition.
The offline bundle contains the page, its images and the PDF. No server is
needed for the browsable page after extracting the bundle.

CONTENTS
6 planet angles; landscape, ground and shelter views; close resident portraits;
13 rock prototypes; 3 sail habits; 3 coral habits; 3 perch forms; 3 animals;
drifting seed; dry stem litter; distant coral representation; shelter model;
3 native saved-find examples; 4 companion-bed channels.
36 source forms are rendered from 2 angles. 24 actual scene/portrait views
are retained. The PDF uses 92 of these render placements.

READING THE IMAGES
World views and portraits are actual application captures from the local
working tree. Studio plates use real source geometry and production material
formulas with fixed light, without aerial haze, post-processing, live
articulation or growth deformation. Shapes are independently fitted, not
displayed at a common scale. Saved finds are nominal source examples.
Young bed scales are included. Distant coral is a simpler representation of
the existing coral population, not another species.
