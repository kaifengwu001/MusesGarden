## Final precision and allocation contract

The physical height cube uses Float32 at 1024, reconstructed with 16-tap Catmull-Rom sampling on its dominant cube face. Edge taps go through normal cube-direction sampling; no face-edge clamp is added. `height0`, `sampleGround` and surface material height share this reconstruction. Flora and all non-height data retain their existing layout. The terrain's conservative relief bound still covers the bounded ridge contribution. Exact worst-sample diagnostics remain in the local parity harness; no test tolerance was enlarged.

The quick height bake remains 256. Golden Rye's custom crop system replaces default near grass and ambient flowers, so their unused pools are eight pages each and default densities are zero. Dry-world cloud reflection cover is 128; the shadow window is 1024. Allocation accounting distinguishes Float32 terrain from half-float flora. See the final validation for measured allocations and accuracy.
