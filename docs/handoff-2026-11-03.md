# Handoff: the night of 2 November 2026

Everything below landed on `main` in one push on the morning of 3 November. It came from the integration branch `muse-visits`, which merged the visits work, Kai's cast, the four world fixes and the `ui` branch. Nothing here was checked by eye: the visual review is Kai's (see "Please look at").

## What changed

### 1. Travelling with your Muse
- **A ticket takes the owner and their Muse to a friend's planet.** Boarding from the post plays the boarding stamp, then opens the host's planet with `?visit=<ticket>&as=<guest>`. Both Muses are there together, and the host's public finds are planted in its ground. A tap on a find opens its card, without like or share. A banner shows the time left and Return home.
- **It happens in the simulated life too.**
  - Using a ticket starts a shared visit for both Muses: "Away at Moss's planet with you" for the guest, "Momo is visiting, with its owner" for the host.
  - The visit ends on Return home or after 2 hours, with a hangout (gifts and a photo, when both still have a photo slot that day).
  - If a Muse is working, the owner goes first and the Muse joins when work ends.
  - The ticket's chart is now sent → used → returned. A second use inside the 2 hours is the same visit; after that the server answers 409.
- **A refused ticket stays home.** The garden says why, and the ticket goes back in the post.
- **Planets not open for visits yet** (Ti Tian Terraces, Ukiyo-e Pine Hamlet) say so; the ticket stays unused.
- **While a trip is open,** the meadow shows "Back to <host>'s planet".
- **Every world takes visits:** Lantern Reed Gardens, Scarlet Sail Canyon, Cobalt Windfields, Tulipwind Meadows, Golden Rye, Cherry Blossom Waterways, Sequoia Shrine, Violet Dunes, and the meadow.
  - Each world opts in with an `environments/<world>/visit.ts` adapter (see environments/README.md, "Taking visits").
  - The guest walks beside its host, and the camera frames the pair side on.
  - A tap on either Muse gets a wave.
  - On the meadow the hut stays shut to visitors.
- **A visitor's rail,** where the garden's rail sits:
  - your Muse's portrait, with "Visiting <Host>'s planet", or "<Muse> is on the way" until the two meet;
  - **Find Muse**, which frames the two Muses;
  - **<Host>'s home**, which goes to the host's cottage, using each world's own home view (`VisitWorld.frameHome`).
  - No basket, post or tickets on a visit. The world's Parameters panel stays.
- **Muses visiting each other show on the planet.** When a friend's Muse visits Momo, it stands beside Momo on the meadow in its own look, with its shadow.
- **Code:** `src/visit/` (shared runtime), `src/meadowVisit.ts`, `src/muse/planets.ts`. Server: `server/engine/engine.ts` (trips), `server/domain/social/invitations.ts`, views and routes (`POST /api/visits/:id/return?as=`). Spec: docs/muse-agent.md.

### 6. The `ui` branch
- Postcards: stills of the meadow picked by what each photo shows, selfies and pastimes, and the backs of postcards (what Muse wrote or drew).
- `T` freezes the moment, with time and camera kept in the URL.
- Each Muse photographs what suits its temperament.
- **Postcard words never repeat, and read funnier.**
  - A Muse remembers its last 30 postcards' words (`recentWords`).
  - The model's prompt asks for more specific, funnier words, and a reply too close to a recent one is turned down.
  - The template pools are much bigger and skip recent words.
- **With `MODEL_API_KEY` set,** the hero's model writes only postcards; judging finds, questions and reflections use the built-in rules. `MUSE_MODEL_MIND=all` hands the model everything. The sim always uses 'all'.

## Demo controls (dev tools)
- With `&dev`, the garden's dev tools have a **Demo** section.
- **Friends:** a checklist of every Muse, to choose exactly who your Muse is friends with.
- **Tickets:** a "Send ticket" button for every Muse, plus "Tickets from all friends". A ticket arrives in the post at once and works like a real one: board, visit, return.
- These are for recording demos; the real ticket rules (after a good hangout, at most one per friend every 14 days) are unchanged.
- Rows for planets not open for visits are disabled. "Tickets from all friends" skips them.
- Dev routes: `POST /api/dev/invite {host, guest}` and `POST /api/dev/friends {muse, friends}`. Both work only on the fast-forward dev clock and answer 409 on the live clock. Fast-forward is the default for `npm run serve`; if you've switched the dev panel to Live, switch it back to Fast-forward to send tickets.

## How to try it
1. **Delete your local garden first:** `rm -rf .muse`. The cast's ids changed, and an old garden still has the old Muses. Then `npm run serve` and `npm run dev`.
2. Open `http://localhost:5173/?garden=/api/muses/hero&dev`. For a demo, use the dev tools' **Demo** section: set Momo's friends and send yourself a ticket from any Muse. Without it, advance the clock until a ticket arrives in the post; Momo's first tickets come from Moss (Violet Dunes) or Hoji (Cherry Blossom Waterways).
3. Open the envelope, then tap the ticket to board. On the host's planet, tap a Muse, tap a find, then Return home.
4. Each world on its own: `/environments/<world>/`. A visit with the fake backend: `npm run test:visit`.
